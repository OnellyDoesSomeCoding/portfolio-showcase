The studio took bookings by email and kept them in a spreadsheet that three people edited at once. Classes were double-booked about once a month.

I built a small site with one page per class and a calendar that shows the free places. A booking writes straight to the database, and the studio gets one email a day with tomorrow's list.

What I would do next: let students move a booking themselves, which is still the most common email the studio gets.
